# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want `ai-check-template` to be a useful, low-friction place for people who care
about safer AI-assisted development.

As maintainers and contributors, we pledge to make participation in this project a
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or
invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality,
personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

We commit to acting and interacting in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that helps create a positive environment include:

- Showing empathy and kindness toward other people.
- Respecting different opinions, experiences, and technical constraints.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them.
- Focusing on what is best for the overall community and project.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting comments, personal attacks, or sustained disruption.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing another person's private information without explicit permission.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces, including:

- GitHub Issues, Pull Requests, Discussions, and reviews.
- Project documentation, examples, and templates.
- Public conversations where someone represents the project or its community.

It also applies when a person is officially representing the project, for example
by using an official project account, presenting on behalf of the project, or
acting as a maintainer in a public venue.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards of acceptable
behavior. They may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits,
Issues, and other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct.

Maintainers should apply these rules consistently and proportionally. They may
also take temporary or permanent action against contributors whose behavior they
judge to be inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it to the maintainers.

For private reports, use GitHub Security Advisories when confidentiality matters.
For public process questions, open an Issue or mention the repository maintainer
by GitHub handle.

All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. Maintainers
will respect the privacy and safety of the reporter as much as possible.

## Enforcement guidelines

Maintainers may use these impact-based guidelines when deciding how to respond.

### 1. Correction

Community impact: use of inappropriate language or other behavior considered
unprofessional or unwelcome.

Consequence: a private or public written warning that explains the violation and
asks for correction. A public apology may be requested when appropriate.

### 2. Warning

Community impact: a single incident or a series of actions that harms the project
environment or another participant.

Consequence: a formal warning. Continued interaction with the people involved may
be limited for a stated period. Violating those limits may lead to a temporary or
permanent ban.

### 3. Temporary ban

Community impact: a serious violation of community standards, including sustained
inappropriate behavior.

Consequence: a temporary ban from project interaction. The contributor may not
participate in project spaces during the ban period.

### 4. Permanent ban

Community impact: a pattern of violating community standards, harassment,
aggression, or conduct that creates safety risk for others.

Consequence: a permanent ban from project spaces and project-controlled
communication channels.
